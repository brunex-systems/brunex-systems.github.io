Accessibility Statement

Effective August 11, 2026

Brunex Systems LLC is committed to providing a website that is usable by and accessible to people with disabilities.

Our approach

We aim to maintain clear page structure, readable text, sufficient color contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation, descriptive link text, text alternatives for meaningful images, responsive layouts, and compatibility with common assistive technologies.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. Technology and standards change, and some content or third-party services may not always perform consistently across every browser, device, or assistive technology. We welcome feedback and will make reasonable efforts to address identified barriers.

Need assistance or want to report a problem?

If you have difficulty accessing any part of this website or need information in another format, please contact us. Include the page or feature involved, the problem encountered, and your preferred way to receive a response.

We will make reasonable efforts to respond and provide the requested information or an accessible alternative.

Standards

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines as a helpful reference when reviewing the site. This statement is not a certification of complete conformance with any particular standard.
